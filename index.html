Who we are

Relaxed mom. Happy baby.
Healthy family.

Mom's Touch brings professional pregnancy, postpartum and baby massage therapy to your doorstep. Every session is designed around what a new mother's body — and a new baby — actually need: gentle pressure, safe natural oils, and a calm space to recover.

Our therapists are trained specifically in maternal and infant massage, so you're never guessing whether a technique is safe for this stage of your journey.
